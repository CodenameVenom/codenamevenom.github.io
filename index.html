About Me

Hi, I'm Giuseppe Benassi, known online as Voldemort. I'm a security consultant, pentester, and IT infrastructure specialist based in Italy.

I work locally with businesses to strengthen their security posture — from vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to designing and building complete IT infrastructures. I help companies set up secure corporate networks, IP surveillance systems, firewalls, VPNs, and server architectures tailored to their needs. I also collaborate with cybersecurity firms to distribute and implement security products (B2B), ensuring organizations get the right tools and configurations to defend their assets.

On the offensive side, I'm active on multiple bug bounty platforms including YesWeHack, HackerOne, and Bugcrowd, with 200+ valid vulnerabilities reported. My approach combines hands-on technical testing with a deep understanding of business risk, whether I'm auditing a web application or wiring a server room.
